# ORBIT 0.10.0 and 0.10.B

**MIDI clock out, quiet drum editing for live sets, a quicker controller
toggle, and Bluetooth that starts on every FM-1.**

## MIDI clock out: GLO > SYSTEM > SYNC = OUT

SYNC has a fourth value. **OUT** runs on ORBIT's own tempo and sends it:

- 24 MIDI clock pulses per beat while playing;
- **START** when you press PLAY and **STOP** when playback stops;
- tempo changes take effect on the next pulse.

Clock goes to USB MIDI, and to Bluetooth MIDI as well in 0.10.B. A DAW,
drum machine or app set to external sync now follows ORBIT's PLAY and
tempo. Swing stays in ORBIT's pattern and isn't added to the clock.

The other values work as before. **INT** uses the internal clock and sends
nothing. **USB** and **TRS** follow an incoming clock and START/CONTINUE/STOP;
Bluetooth MIDI input arrives on the USB route, so use **USB** to follow a
Bluetooth clock. ORBIT doesn't forward an incoming clock, the FM-1 has no
TRS MIDI output, and CONTINUE and Song Position aren't sent.

## Drum GRID editing that stays quiet

Drawing a beat in front of an audience shouldn't play every hit as you place
it. On the drum track's **GRID** page:

- **White keys** add or remove the selected voice's hits **silently**. A new
  hit sounds only when the playhead reaches it, so the groove isn't broken
  while you edit.
- **KNOB 1** (voice) and **KNOB 2** (step cursor) move silently too.
- **Black keys** still select a voice and let you hear it.
- **With REC on while playing,** a black key also **records** its voice at
  the moment you hit it, like a live drum key. Pick a sound, check it, and
  play it straight into the pattern. White keys keep toggling steps quietly.

KNOB 1 can reach all 16 voices, but only 11 have black keys. Use **KIT** to
play the other five live.

## MIDI controller mode: tap OCT- + OCT+

Controller mode now toggles with one left-hand gesture: tap **OCT-** and
**OCT+** together (press both at once and let go within about half a
second, with no other button or key). This replaces the 2-second ENV + LFO
hold, and **MENU > AUDIO > CONTROLLER** still works.

Longer holds keep their old jobs. The 5-second update-mode hold, ghost/hard
accents on the drum track and two separate octave presses don't toggle the
mode. Tapping both also resets the octave to 0, as it always has.

## Storage

**ORP8 projects, OPB3 USER banks, the `step_t` layout and the 104-byte
settings object are unchanged.** SYNC = OUT uses a spare value of the
existing setting. Older firmware reads it as INT.

## Validation

The host suite, target builds and budgets, native preview and web tests
were rerun for this release; new host tests cover the clock output, the
controller tap, quiet GRID editing and black-key recording. Development
builds with these changes were installed on an FM-1, where the device
owner confirmed that they work, including clock out. Details and package
hashes are in the
[validation record](../VALIDATION.md#orbit-0100-and-010b-release-2026-10-31).
Clock jitter and drift weren't measured with instruments.

Earlier changes are in the [0.9.0 notes](0.9.0.md).
